and everything those import, under the same paths without the src/ prefix. These files are never edited here
(SHA256SUMS holds their hashes; `npm run check:ren` verifies them). `node scripts/sync-ren.mjs <ren desktop checkout> .`
copies the import closure of those entries, type imports included, and writes SHA256SUMS. ren desktop's `@catalyst`
alias is in vite.config.ts and tsconfig.ren.json.

The phone's mounts supply what Electron supplies on the desktop (src/host.ts: the workspace token, the favorites, the
ren account the pages are signed in to, and the app's events):
